**Headline: Rescuing Quick-Commerce: How We Built NOVA PULSE for PromptWars 2026 ⚡🛒**

---

Most quick-commerce startups think growth is just about spending more on acquisition. 

In PromptWars 2026, we were presented with **NOVA CART** — a hyper-local platform connecting 620 local stores across 3 Indian cities. On paper, it was growing: 1.20L users and ₹26.1L monthly revenue.

**Under the hood, it was bleeding:**
📉 Repeat purchase rate plummeted from 41% to 27%.
🚫 Cancellations doubled to 11% (over 4,235 orders lost each month).
⏳ Average delivery time worsened by 28% to 37 minutes.
🔥 Monthly promo spend ballooned to ₹17.0 Lakh (65% of revenue!).
💔 Most shocking of all: **61% of churned customers had previously rated the app 4★ or higher.**

They didn't leave because they disliked local stores — they left because of broken operational promises: **"Phantom Inventory"** (items shown in-stock running out post-payment) and disconnected support systems taking **9.2 hours** to resolve refunds.

### Enter NOVA PULSE ⚡
We built and deployed **NOVA PULSE** — an AI-powered local commerce rescue engine designed strictly under the ₹25 Lakh budget constraint:

1️⃣ **Zero-Friction Merchant Copilot**: Kirana and bakery owners update stock in 0.2s with a 1-Tap Toggle or speak informal voice notes ("Got 20 units of farm milk, sourdough sold out"). Powered by **Google Gemini API**, it extracts structured catalog updates instantly, generating a live **Stock Confidence Index (SCI)** that kills phantom inventory.
2️⃣ **Multi-Store Neighborhood Bundles**: Combines artisan sourdough, organic milk, and pharmacy essentials into one route-clustered delivery, driving users straight to the proven **3-Order / 72% Repeat Retention Threshold**.
3️⃣ **Autonomous Support & Instant Refunds**: Slashes customer ticket resolution from 9.2 hours to **~4.2 seconds** with automated UPI refunds and proactive delay credits.
4️⃣ **Interactive Executive ROI Console**: Recovers **₹8.4 Lakh / month** in saved marketing burn and prevented cancellations, while spending just ₹14.8L of the allowed ₹25L 6-month budget.

### Technology Stack:
* Frontend: React 18, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons
* Backend: Python 3.13, Flask, SQLite, Gunicorn
* AI: Google Gemini API (v1beta / Gemini 1.5 Flash)
* Deployment: Vercel & Render
