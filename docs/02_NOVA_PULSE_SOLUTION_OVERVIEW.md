## Part A: Problem Understanding & Diagnostic Audit

### The "Leaky Bucket" Crisis
NOVA CART expanded rapidly across 3 Indian cities, reaching **1,20,000 registered users** and **620 partner stores**. However, forensic analysis of the platform revealed that it was pouring expensive customer acquisition spend into a deeply cracked operational funnel.

While gross monthly revenue reached ₹26.1 Lakh, monthly promotional discounting burn ballooned to **₹17.0 Lakh** (consuming 65.1% of gross revenue). More alarmingly, **58% of this marketing spend was burned on one-time coupon redeemers** who immediately churned. The platform suffered from an acute operational paradox: **61% of churned users had previously rated the service 4 stars or higher**, indicating that customer attrition was not caused by lack of demand, but by broken operational reliability.

## Part B: NOVA PULSE Solution Concept & Architecture

### Core Strategic Thesis
Traditional quick-commerce giants react to operational strain by burning tens of millions of dollars on company-owned dark stores, proprietary vehicle fleets, and deep subsidies. For local commerce aggregators, this approach leads directly to insolvency.

**NOVA PULSE** implements an **Asset-Light Digital Orchestration Strategy**:
1. It does not replace neighborhood kiranas with dark warehouses; it empowers existing shopkeepers with ultra-low-friction mobile tools.
2. It does not deploy massive delivery fleets; it clusters multi-store local pickups within unified geographical radii.
3. It stops burning ₹17L/month on indiscriminate discounts; it redirects capital into a structured **3-Order Retention Ladder** that systematically guides customers to the 72% repeat threshold.

## Part E: Comprehensive Feature-by-Feature Documentation

### 1. Customer Portal
The Customer Portal (`CustomerStoreView.jsx`) provides an intuitive, high-speed shopping experience designed to build trust and eliminate pre-checkout friction:
- **Neighborhood Store & Category Discovery:** Customers can browse verified local stores across categories (Organic Produce, Bakery, Dairy, Kirana, Pharmacy) with live ratings, delivery estimates, and address badges.
- **Stock Confidence Index (SCI) Badge:** Every store displays a real-time color-coded badge:
  - **Green (90%–100%):** *Guaranteed Live Stock* (recently verified).
  - **Yellow (75%–89%):** *Fresh Stock* (verified within 2–6 hours).
  - **Red (< 75%):** *Stale Stock* (prompting real-time verification).
- **Curated Multi-Store Bundles:** Bundles such as *"Healthy Breakfast Kit"* (combining items from *Green Valley Organics* and *Daily Bakehouse*) or *"Evening Chai & Snacks"* allow customers to purchase from multiple stores in a single order, saving money and delivery fees.
- **Low-Stock & Low-SCI Pre-Checkout Guard:** When a customer attempts to checkout with an item having `stock_quantity <= 2` or belonging to a store with `SCI < 80%`, the system automatically presents an interactive confirmation modal:
  > *"Live Inventory Verification: One or more items in your cart have limited stock. The store will prioritize immediate dispatch upon checkout. Would you like to proceed?"*
- **Server-Side Inventory Revalidation:** The backend API (`POST /api/orders`) atomically re-verifies inventory availability at the moment of checkout, rejecting stale checkouts with a 400 error rather than accepting payment and cancelling later.
- **Unified Order Tracking & History:** Customers can track active orders through four clear status stages:
  1. `placed`: Order received by platform.
  2. `preparing`: Stores are packaging items.
  3. `ready`: Items packaged and staged for rider dispatch.
  4. `completed`: Successfully delivered.
- **Order-Specific Issue Reporting:** From the order history view, customers can submit dispute tickets directly linked to their `order_id` (e.g., Damaged Item, Missing Product, Delayed Delivery).

### 2. Merchant Copilot
The Merchant Copilot (`MerchantCopilot.jsx`) gives neighborhood shopkeepers a friction-free operational cockpit designed for mobile screens:
- **Real-Time Store Health & SCI Meter:** Displays the store's current Stock Confidence Score, orders processed today, and the timestamp of the last stock verification.
- **1-Tap Quick Stock Toggles:** Shopkeepers can toggle fast-depleting staples (e.g., Milk, Eggs, Bread) between *In Stock*, *Low Stock*, and *Out of Stock* with a single tap on their phone screen.
- **Gemini AI Voice & Text Inventory Updater:** Shopkeepers can type or speak natural language updates (e.g., *"We just received 15 kg of Sharbati Atta and Amul Butter is out of stock"*). The AI extracts the SKU, quantity, and status, and updates the database immediately.
- **Rush Mode Toggle:** During heavy evening in-store walk-in surges, merchants can activate **Rush Mode** with one tap. This automatically adds a **+15 minute buffer** to customer delivery estimates, preventing store panic and order rejections without needing to close the digital storefront.
- **Incoming Store Orders View with Item Isolation:** For multi-store bundle orders, the merchant sees **only their store's line items**. The merchant can independently progress their items from `placed` to `preparing` and `ready` without affecting the other store's items.

### 4. Stock Confidence Index (SCI) Algorithmic Model
The Stock Confidence Index is an algorithmic trust score calculated dynamically based on elapsed time since the merchant's last inventory verification.

#### Implementation in `backend/services/inventory_service.py`:
```python
def calculate_sci(last_sync_timestamp):
    now = datetime.datetime.now()
    delta_hours = (now - last_sync_timestamp).total_seconds() / 3600.0

    if delta_hours <= 2.0:
        # High confidence: 98% - 99%
        return max(90, int(99 - (delta_hours * 1.5)))
    elif delta_hours <= 6.0:
        # Fresh stock: 92% - 97%
        return max(85, int(95 - ((delta_hours - 2.0) * 2.0)))
    elif delta_hours <= 24.0:
        # Moderate decay: 80% - 91%
        return max(70, int(87 - ((delta_hours - 6.0) * 1.0)))
    else:
        # Stale inventory: decaying to baseline 45%
        return max(45, int(69 - ((delta_hours - 24.0) * 0.5)))
```

When a merchant taps "Confirm All In-Stock" or updates any item, `last_inventory_sync` updates to `datetime.now()`, instantly restoring their SCI score to **98%–99%**.

### 5. Gemini AI Engine & Deterministic Fallback

#### Gemini 1.5 Flash Integration:
- In `backend/services/gemini_service.py`, requests are dispatched to Google Gemini's REST API endpoint:  
  `https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/v1beta/models/gemini-1.5-flash:generateContent?key={GEMINI_API_KEY}`
- It operates with a temperature of `0.1` and explicit system instructions demanding strict JSON output schema.
- **Voice Inventory Parsing:** Converts unstructured speech/text transcripts into structured inventory mutation payloads (`[{"product_name": "...", "stock_quantity": N, "is_in_stock": 1}]`).
- **Autonomous Dispute Triage:** Evaluates customer issue descriptions, categorizes severity, and authorizes simulated refunds up to ₹500 according to platform rules.

#### Deterministic Fallback Engine:
If `GEMINI_API_KEY` is not provided, expired, or network requests fail:
1. The service intercepts the error gracefully without raising 500 exceptions.
2. A deterministic regular expression and keyword parser processes the input:
   - Identifies keywords: `milk`, `bread`, `eggs`, `atta`, `curd`, `butter`, etc.
   - Extracts quantities: `(\d+)\s*(kg|packets|bottles|units)?`.
   - Recognizes status phrases: `out of stock`, `finished`, `sold out`, `khatam`, `depleting`.
3. In customer dispute triage, keyword rules categorize tickets (`damaged`, `missing`, `late`, `quality`), calculate standard compensation, and return structured JSON with:
   `is_simulated_payment: true` and `ai_confidence: 0.85 (deterministic fallback)`.

## Part G: Limitations, Simulated Disclosures, and Future Roadmap

### 1. Simulated Payment & Refund Disclosures
In strict accordance with hackathon competition safety and financial compliance rules:
- **All UPI payment settlements and customer refunds within NOVA PULSE are simulated.**
- When a customer support ticket is resolved with an instant refund, the API explicitly returns `is_simulated_payment: true` and records the simulated transaction in the audit ledger. No real banking or UPI networks are debited or credited.

### 2. Current Architecture Boundaries
- **Single-Branch Architecture:** All improvements are maintained on the `main` branch under the 10 MB repository cap.
- **SQLite Concurrency:** SQLite is ideal for demonstration and local evaluation. For a live deployment processing 50,000 concurrent requests, the relational schema is designed to migrate effortlessly to PostgreSQL with zero schema refactoring.
- **Role Switcher:** The top navigation bar includes a hackathon role switcher (`Customer`, `Merchant`, `Admin`) to allow evaluators to inspect all 3 journeys from one browser session without credential management friction.

### 3. Future Roadmap
1. **ONDC Beckn Protocol Integration:** Enable external ONDC buyer apps (Paytm, Mystore, Magicpin) to discover NOVA PULSE bundles natively.
2. **WhatsApp Business API Webhook:** Allow Kirana merchants to update stock by sending simple voice notes directly to a verified WhatsApp Business number.
3. **Automated Rider Batching:** Coordinated dual-pickup routing for multi-store bundles using real-time open-source routing algorithms (OSRM).
